Talk about ERP & Business Automation

Most ERP failures are not software failures. They happen when a company is forced to work the way a foreign template assumes it does. We map your real process first — including the parts that live in a register book — and configure or build to match it.

Then we automate what should never have been manual: approvals, reconciliations, attendance, payroll runs, purchase requisitions, compliance reports.

What the work includes

  • Process mapping and requirements workshops
  • Finance, inventory, procurement and production modules
  • HRM: attendance, leave, payroll and appraisals
  • Workflow and approval automation with audit trails
  • Manufacturing execution and shop-floor data capture
  • Data migration from spreadsheets and legacy systems
  • Role-based access control and compliance reporting
  • Rollout, change management and floor-level training

What you receive

  • Configured ERP/HRM with your process encoded
  • Migrated master data with reconciliation report
  • Role-based training for every user group
  • Phase-two automation backlog with business cases
