A furniture storefront where the delivery promise is finally true

A rebuilt commerce experience joining showroom stock, warehouse inventory and courier capacity into a single, honest delivery date.

The challenge

Aurelia sold furniture online with a generic "7–14 days" promise that was wrong more often than right. Twenty-nine percent of orders were cancelled after the customer was told the real date by phone.

The product pages could not convey scale or material, so returns ran high and the showroom team fielded the same five questions on every call.

How we approached it

01

Make the promise computable

We modelled the actual constraint chain — showroom stock, warehouse stock, assembly lead time, courier capacity by district — so the page can state a date it can keep.

02

Rebuild the product page

Scale references, room context, material close-ups and dimensions in a form people can check against their own space, all rendered from a single asset pipeline.

03

Checkout that fits the category

Split payment, instalments, scheduled delivery windows and an assembly option, because furniture is not a two-tap purchase.

04

SEO that compounds

Category and buying-guide content structured around how people actually search for furniture in Bangla and English.

What we built

  • Delivery date computed from stock, assembly and courier capacity
  • Product pages with scale references, dimensions and material detail
  • Instalment and split-payment checkout with MFS and card support
  • Scheduled delivery windows with assembly booking
  • Showroom stock visible online with reserve-in-store
  • Structured buying guides driving organic category traffic
Results

Measured against the baseline we agreed at kickoff.
